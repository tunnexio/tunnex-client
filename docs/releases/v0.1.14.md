# Tunnex Desktop v0.1.14

## Local Sharing (LS-CLIENT-1)

Plain-language Local Sharing in the desktop app, matching the web console's terms.
Decisions are recorded in `docs/LS-CLIENT-1-local-sharing.md`.

- Reviewers are shown as "Name (email)" and groups as "Developers group", with
  "· N members" when the control plane reports it. You are no longer offered as
  your own reviewer, and share cards list reviewers instead of "audience rules".
- Durations read correctly ("1 hour", "1 hour 30 minutes").
- A visible reason explains why Create share, New share or Extend is unavailable:
  a missing step, your organization's share limit or its maximum lifetime.
- My shares now lists paused, connecting and offline shares, so Resume and
  Reconnect can be reached. Ended shares still leave the list at expiry.
- People who can't publish, and organizations where Local Sharing is off or its
  setup is unfinished, see a plain policy message instead of an error.
- Stop is confirmed in the app. The "App access revoked" notice for a withdrawn
  review now says "Shared app no longer available".
- The first time you close the window while sharing, a one-time notice explains
  that sharing continues in the tray until you quit.
- "Shared by Name (email)" needs a control plane that includes the
  `publisher_email` projection (after v0.1.50). Older servers show the name alone.

## Privilege helper (PR #24)

If PR #24 is merged before this tag, this release also carries its macOS helper
fix: the helper socket stays reachable after a reboot ("connect EACCES
/var/run/tunnex/helper.sock"). The daemon log moves to
`/Library/Logs/Tunnex/helper.log`, and the helper owns and verifies its runtime
directory. If #24 is not merged at tag time, remove this section.

Both manifests are 0.1.14. Publish only through the existing tag workflow after
checks pass on the merged head.
